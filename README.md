<h3>🚀 Featured Projects</h3>

<p>
An end-to-end insurance claims fraud detection and risk scoring platform that analyzes claims data and identifies suspicious patterns using machine learning.
</p>

<p>
Built with Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Next.js, TypeScript, Pandas, and Isolation Forest, with analytics and risk scoring designed for practical fraud investigation.
</p>

<h4>⚖️ Court Case Tracking & Monitoring System</h4>

<p>
A Python-based system designed to monitor and process court case information from public court data sources.
</p>

<p>
Handles case tracking, web extraction, display board monitoring, cause-list processing, JSON-based case management, periodic status checks, and automated email notifications.
</p>

<h4>🏙️ URBIX — Urban Data & Intelligence Platform</h4>

<p>
A data-driven platform focused on understanding and analyzing urban information through interactive data and APIs.

<p>
Works with urban data, environmental information, rainfall and weather data, visualization, and decision-support concepts using Python, React, TypeScript, and APIs.
</p>

A modern application focused on managing and presenting healthcare-related information through structured data and API-based communication.
